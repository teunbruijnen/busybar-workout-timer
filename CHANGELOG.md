# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-07)

The first public release of the JavaScript app.

- Runs on the bar itself: Tabata, fixed rest and fixed work, set up with the wheel and buttons, with BUSY's timer sounds and a recap.
- Get ready, Sound, Time step and Skip with are on the bar's Setup page.
- The colon of a time stays on one column, so nothing shifts as the minutes change width.
- A step back in the bar's clock, or a jump forward of more than 10 s, such as a time sync, doesn't move a workout.
